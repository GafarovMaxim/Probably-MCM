[b]🎮 For Players:[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]All your mods in one place:[/b] Accessible right from the Pause Menu ([b]Esc[/b]), the Main Menu, or via the [b]F10[/b] hotkey.
[*] [b]Live Keybinding:[/b] Hotkey rebinding with modifier support ([b]Ctrl[/b], [b]Alt[/b], [b]Shift[/b] + key). Press [b]Delete[/b] to unbind.
[*] [b]Fast & sleek interface:[/b] Instant search, settings counter, and neat layout in the game's native style.
[*] [b]Works right out of the box:[/b] Existing mods with standard configs appear in the menu automatically.
[*] [b]Instant changes:[/b] Settings apply on the fly, with a one-click reset to defaults if you ever want to revert.
[*] [b]Native language:[/b] Fully localized into all 9 in-game languages.
[/list]

[b]🛠️ For Mod Authors:[/b]
Make your mod enjoyable to configure. Use the game's native [i]ModConfig[/i] for zero-dependency auto-detection, or use our Fluent API with custom sections, tooltips, and live callbacks.
