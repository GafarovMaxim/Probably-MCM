[b]🎮 What it does for you:[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]All your mods in one place:[/b] Accessible right from the Pause Menu ([b]Esc[/b]), the Main Menu, or via the [b]F10[/b] hotkey.
[*] [b]Effortless Keybinding:[/b] No more typing raw key codes. Click a hotkey setting, press your desired key or combo ([b]Ctrl[/b], [b]Alt[/b], [b]Shift[/b] + Key), and you're done. Clear it with [b]Delete[/b], cancel with [b]Esc[/b].
[*] [b]Clean, fast interface:[/b] Instant search bar, settings counter, and neat layout that fits right into the game's style.
[*] [b]Works right out of the box:[/b] Existing mods with standard configs appear automatically — no manual setup needed.
[*] [b]Rich controls:[/b] Smooth sliders, toggles, exact numbers, dropdowns, and action buttons.
[*] [b]Instant changes:[/b] Tweaks apply on the fly and save automatically, with a one-click reset to defaults if you ever need it.
[*] [b]Full translation:[/b] Translated into all 9 in-game languages.
[/list]

[b]🛠️ For Mod Authors:[/b]
Make your mod enjoyable to configure. Use the game's native [i]ModConfig[/i] for zero-dependency auto-detection, or use our lightweight Fluent API for custom sections, hover tooltips, and live callbacks.
